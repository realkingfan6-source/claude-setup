# stop-slop

Idea from a reel by @krishnagangwal.ai showing Hardik Pandya's "Stop Slop" skill (2026-10). Rewritten for J's
projects; nothing copied.

## When
Any text a stranger reads: scripts (the AI narrator reads them aloud), titles, descriptions, listing copy, blurbs,
captions, in-game UI text. Run it after drafting and before the Critic.

For longer prose (scripts, descriptions, listings, blurbs), also run the `humanizer` skill (blader, 26 patterns from
Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing", installed 2026-10-20). stop-slop = J's house rules; humanizer = the deep pattern pass.

## 1. Strip the tells
Rewrite on sight:
- Contrast templates: "it's not X, it's Y", "not just X, but Y", "X? No. Y."
- Throat-clearing: "here's the thing", "let's dive in", "in today's video", "buckle up", "picture this".
- Reassurance filler: "and that's okay", "you're not alone", "trust me".
- Fake reveals: "plot twist", "but here's the catch", when what follows isn't surprising.
- Inflated words: delve, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer, journey,
  navigate (when nobody is steering anything), vibrant, robust.
- Em dashes. Use a period or a comma (YouTube titles: no colons either, J's rule).
- Reflex threes: three adjectives, three-beat lists, when one or two would do.
- Runs of same-length sentences; every paragraph ending on a punchline.
- Stacked hedges: "might potentially", "can often help".
- Summary endings: "in conclusion", "at the end of the day", "ultimately".
- Decorative emoji; an exclamation mark on every line.

## 2. Score it (1-10 each, 50 max)
| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Directness | Does the first sentence say the thing? Statements, not announcements of statements. |
| Rhythm | Do sentence lengths vary? Does it read well out loud? |
| Trust | Is every claim checkable, every number sourced, nothing overpromised? |
| Authenticity | Would a real person say this to a friend? Specific details over generic ones? |
| Density | Is anything cuttable? Cut it. |

Under 35: rewrite and score again. Show J the score only if asked; otherwise hand over the clean version.

## 3. Rewrite rules
- Concrete beats abstract: a number, a name, a place.
- Say it once. Delete the sentence that repeats the one before it.
- Read it aloud once (or through the narrator) and fix where you stumble.

## Never
- Change facts, numbers or quotes, or invent a detail or personal story to sound real.
- Use this to hide AI use where a platform asks for disclosure (Etsy, KDP, YouTube altered content). Disclosure stays.

Last line of every run: **ship it, or change it?**
